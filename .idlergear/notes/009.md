Enhanced benchmark with smart event-based updates

**Update triggers** (whichever happens first):
1. ⏰ Every 1 minute (time-based)
2. 🔢 Every 5 queries (milestones)
3. 💾 Every 10 queries (checkpoints)
4. ⚠️ Slow queries (2x avg latency)
5. 📈 Memory spikes (>100MB increase)
6. ✅ Completion

**Example output**:
```
[5/50] Latency: 125ms | Recall: 0.850 | Memory: 2048MB | ETA: 5m 30s
[7/50] Latency: 132ms | Recall: 0.845 | Memory: 2049MB | ETA: 5m 15s | ⚠ slow query: 287ms
[10/50] Latency: 118ms | Recall: 0.862 | Memory: 2051MB | ETA: 4m 45s | 💾 checkpoint
[18/50] Latency: 117ms | Recall: 0.865 | Memory: 2342MB | ETA: 3m 45s | 📈 mem spike: +289MB
```

**Benefits**:
- Never wait >1 min for update
- Catch slow queries immediately
- Detect memory leaks as they happen
- See meaningful events, not just fixed intervals
