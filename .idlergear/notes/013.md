Slot retriever benchmark analysis (20260103_081055)

**4 retrievers tested on 20 queries, 4.2M docs:**

1. **MemoryMappedSlotRetriever** (Solution 1)
   - Recall@10: 90% (BEST accuracy!)
   - Latency: 44.8ms mean (SLOWEST)
   - Memory: 676MB peak (BEST memory)
   - Trade-off: Slow due to disk I/O

2. **FAISSSlotRetriever** (Solution 2)
   - Recall@10: 85%
   - Latency: 5.1ms mean (8.7x faster than mmap)
   - Memory: 683MB peak
   - Problem: Suboptimal FAISS config (nlist=√N, flat quantizer)

3. **MultiFAISSSlotRetriever** (Solution 3)
   - Recall@10: 75% (WORST accuracy)
   - Latency: 1.1ms mean (FASTEST!)
   - Memory: 683MB peak
   - Problem: Intersection too strict, loses recall

4. **SQLiteSlotRetriever** (Solution 4)
   - Recall@10: 85%
   - Latency: 30.9ms mean, 158ms p99 (high variance)
   - Memory: 676MB peak
   - Problem: Slow, unpredictable latency

**Key insights:**
- Mmap has best accuracy but too slow for production
- FAISS is promising but needs optimization (nlist tuning, HNSW quantizer)
- MultiFAISS is fastest but needs weighted fusion instead of intersection
- SQLite has latency variance issues
