## Topical Model Validation Results

**Model:** `models/topical_embeddings/best_model.pt`
**Trained:** 2026-01-05 23:28
**Training data:** 8.66M pairs (topical_pairs_smart.jsonl)

### Training Metrics

- **Epochs:** 10
- **Final loss:** 0.0358
- **Vocabulary:** 77,236 roots (vs 11,121 linguistic roots)
- **Embedding dimension:** 64d
- **Training progression:**
  - Epoch 1: 0.1448
  - Epoch 5: 0.0507 (good convergence)
  - Epoch 10: 0.0358 (final)

### Semantic Quality Assessment

**Test results: 11/15 passed (73%) - FAIR quality**

**Strong performance (✓):**
- Animals: hund↔kat (0.600), hund↔leon (0.481), fiŝ↔bird (0.601), tigr↔leon (0.512)
- Nature: arb↔flor (0.631)
- Celestial: sun↔lun (0.464)
- Science: fizik↔kemi (0.476)
- Geography: franc↔german (0.503), eŭrop↔azi (0.404)
- Unrelated pairs correctly low: pariz↔fiŝ (0.289), sun↔tabel (0.135)

**Weak performance (⚠/MISS):**
- Cities: pariz↔london (0.307) - **too low, should be >0.4**
- Subjects: histori↔geografi (0.336) - **too low**
- Missing: matemat↔geometri (N/A) - **'geometri' not in vocab**
- Missing: hund↔matemat (N/A) - **'matemat' not in vocab**

### Key Issues Identified

1. **Proper noun handling:**
   - Some proper nouns (pariz, eŭrop) exist in BOTH linguistic and topical vocabs
   - Should be topical-only for semantic consistency
   - This explains why pariz↔london similarity is lower than expected

2. **Vocabulary gaps:**
   - 'geometri', 'matemat' missing from 77K vocab
   - These are important academic terms
   - Suggests vocabulary extraction may have missed some common roots

3. **Embedding space statistics:**
   - Topical mean similarity: 0.040 (reasonable)
   - Topical std deviation: 0.133 (good spread)
   - Range: [-0.483, 0.625] (healthy range)

### Comparison: Topical vs Linguistic

**Topical strengths:**
- Better at contextual/topic-based similarity (hund↔manĝ: 0.554 topical vs 0.010 linguistic)
- Good for animals, nature, science domains
- 7x larger vocabulary (77K vs 11K)

**Linguistic strengths:**
- Better at morphological relationships (bel↔ĉarm: 0.071 linguistic, but captures adjectival pattern)
- More consistent for core Fundamento roots

### Overall Assessment

**Status:** ✅ **USABLE** but has room for improvement

**Strengths:**
- Converged well (loss 0.1448 → 0.0358)
- Strong on animal/nature/science domains
- 77K vocabulary provides good coverage
- Works well in hybrid mode (11/15 tests passed)

**Weaknesses:**
- Some proper noun pairs underperform (cities: 0.307 when should be >0.4)
- Vocabulary gaps for academic terms
- Some proper nouns wrongly in both vocabs

### Recommendations

**Short-term:** Use as-is for hybrid embeddings
- 73% accuracy is acceptable for initial deployment
- Hybrid mode will compensate with linguistic embeddings
- Good enough to benchmark and validate approach

**Medium-term improvements:**
1. Fix vocabulary extraction to capture all academic/technical terms
2. Clean proper noun classification (topical-only, not both)
3. Add more city/geography pairs to training data
4. Consider training for 15-20 epochs (loss still decreasing)

**Long-term:** See Task #83 - train affix transforms for 128d
