## Keyword Prefilter vs Semantic Search Trade-off

The improved keyword prefilter now requires ALL significant terms (issue #95 fix), but this creates a semantic gap:

**Example**: "Kiu fondis Esperanton?" (Who founded Esperanto?)
- Keyword prefilter requires: "fond" + "esperant"
- MISS: "ZAMENHOF, Aŭtoro de la lingvo Esperanto" (no "fond")
- HIT: "li tuj fondis firmaeton Interland" (has "fond" + "Esperant" but wrong answer)

**Root Cause**: Keyword prefilter is too literal. It can't bridge:
- "fondis" (founded) ↔ "aŭtoro" (author)
- "fondis" (founded) ↔ "kreinto" (creator)

**Options to fix**:
1. Use semantic relations from ReVo to expand keywords (fondis → kreinto, aŭtoro)
2. Fall back to HNSW when keyword prefilter returns too few results
3. Use hybrid approach: keyword candidates + semantic candidates merged

The slot-based reranking helps but can't fix recall issues from the prefilter stage.
