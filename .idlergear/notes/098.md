### Remaining Issues

**Core Problem**: "ZAMENHOF, Aŭtoro de la lingvo Esperanto" exists in index (position 18) but is NOT surfaced in top 50 results.

**Root Cause**: Semantic gap between:
- Query verb: "fondis" (founded)
- Document noun: "Aŭtoro" (author)

The embeddings don't capture that "author of a language" ≈ "founder of a language". This requires:
1. Better semantic relations (fond ↔ aŭtor connection)
2. Or entity-based retrieval that boosts docs with matching entities regardless of verb
