## Semantic Embedding Quality Analysis

### Current State (Topical Embeddings)
Skip-gram trained on sliding windows captures distributional similarity but NOT semantic similarity:

**Good** (common words, many training examples):
- hund ↔ kat: 0.60
- manĝ ↔ trink: 0.50

**Bad** (rare/proper nouns, specialized terms):
- esperant ↔ lingv: 0.008 (should be ~0.8)
- fond ↔ kre: 0.26 (should be ~0.9)
- zamenhof ↔ esperant: 0.18 (should be ~0.7)

### Root Cause
Skip-gram learns: "words appearing in similar contexts have similar embeddings"
We need: "words with similar meanings have similar embeddings"

### Available Training Data for Improvement
1. SemanticRelationDB synonyms: 2,598 sets
2. Curated synonyms: 50+ verified pairs
3. ReVo hypernyms: 2,794 relations
4. ReVo hyponyms: 425 relations

### Proposed Solution
Contrastive learning with triplet loss:
- (anchor, positive=synonym, negative=random)
- Directly optimizes for semantic similarity
