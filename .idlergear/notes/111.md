## Topical Embeddings Evaluation Findings (2026-01-08)

**Issue**: #243 - Poor cross-topic discrimination

**Key insight**: 87.6% validation accuracy on binary co-occurrence task does NOT mean good embeddings.

The model learned to predict "do these words appear together?" but the embedding space has everything clustered together (0.9+ similarity across unrelated topics).

**Why this happens**: Binary classification only needs to separate positive from negative pairs. It doesn't need embeddings to be spread out - a model could put all positive pairs at similarity 0.95 and all negatives at 0.90 and still get 87% accuracy.

**Better approaches**:
1. **Triplet loss**: Explicitly optimizes margin between positive and negative
2. **Contrastive loss with temperature**: Pushes negatives further apart
3. **Hard negative mining**: Sample negatives that are confusingly similar

**Lesson**: Always evaluate embedding QUALITY separately from training accuracy.
